A chatbot can describe cities. It can't check your deadline.

General-purpose LLMs answer study-abroad questions from memory. TasteCompass is an agent: it calls Qloo for taste, reads official course data for facts, and only plans from what it can source.

From what you love to a plan with dates.

Start wherever you are. Undecided on everything, or already set on two programmes, the agent picks the right tools for your situation.

  1. Say what's decided

    Nothing yet, the country, a city, or specific programmes. Whatever you fix stays fixed.

  2. Share your tastes

    Artists, films, books, food and hobbies. Qloo turns them into a taste profile and scores every major student city.

  3. Weigh what matters

    Programmes are scored on taste, academic fit, budget and English requirements. Drag the weights to match your priorities.

  4. Get a dated plan

    For each pick: when to book IELTS, ask referees and submit, worked back from the real deadline.

Qloo does the part no chatbot can.

Without Qloo, TasteCompass would be another search box. With it, "where would I be happy?" becomes something you can measure.

Understands your taste

Each interest is matched in Qloo's taste graph. Works and people become entities; activities become tags.

Compares cities on one map

One Qloo heatmap covers the whole UK, so every city is scored on the same scale, measured within 10 km of its centre.

For the example tastes, Bath and Bristol rank near the top; London sits near the bottom.

Shows what you'd love there

Qloo's place recommendations, filtered to the city and cleaned of national chains, become a first week you'd actually enjoy.
